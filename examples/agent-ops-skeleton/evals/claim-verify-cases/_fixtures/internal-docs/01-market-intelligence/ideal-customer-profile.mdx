# ICP (fixture stub)

Platform teams at mid-size logistics and e-commerce companies that must run
background jobs inside their own network, issue worker credentials from
their own identity provider, and pass audits that ask "who could enqueue or
consume this job?". Evaluation friction matters: time-to-first-job under
five minutes, no procurement step to evaluate.
